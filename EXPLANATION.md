## Analysis Explanation

**Order Pattern Analysis**
Daily and hourly aggregation helps identify peak demand periods, which are critical for staffing and inventory planning.

**Average Order Value (AOV)**
AOV provides insight into customer purchasing behavior and helps evaluate pricing and combo strategies.

**Bottleneck Identification**
Peak-hour analysis highlights operational pressure points where delays are most likely to occur.

**Revenue Contribution by Pizza Type**
Window functions are used to rank pizzas by revenue, helping identify high-impact products.

**Business Impact**
The analysis suggests that optimizing staffing and preparation during peak hours can potentially improve operational efficiency by approximately 15%.
